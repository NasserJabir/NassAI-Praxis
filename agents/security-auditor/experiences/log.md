# Security Audit Experience Log

## Format
Each entry should include:
- **Date**: When the audit was conducted
- **Subject**: What was audited
- **Approach**: Audit methodology used
- **Outcome**: Results achieved
- **Quality**: Self-assessment (1-5)
- **Lessons**: What was learned

---

- **Subject**: [Description of audit subject]
- **Audit type**: [Static/Dynamic/Manual/Automated]
- **Approach**: [Audit methodology used]
- **Vulnerabilities found**: [Number by severity]
- **Time spent**: [Duration]
- **Quality**: [1-5 rating]
- **Lessons learned**: [Key insights]
- **Recommendations**: [Key findings]
- **Remediation status**: [Pending/In Progress/Resolved]
